A Local's Guide to Munich

Curated tips for Wombat's City Hostel guests and Munich explorers.

📍 Arriving at Hauptbahnhof

Welcome to Munich! The hostel is just a 5-minute walk from the station. Pro tip: Use the North exit to avoid the busiest crowds.

View on Map →

🍺 Beer Gardens & Food

Augustiner-Keller is nearby and a total classic. Don't forget to try a Brezn (pretzel) with Obatzda cheese!

Book a Food Tour →

🚇 Getting Around

Buy a "Group Day Ticket" if you're 2+ people—it's much cheaper. Use the MVGO app for real-time schedules.

🏰 Essential Sights

Marienplatz is a must, but if you want a view, climb St. Peter's Church (Alter Peter). It's cheaper than the Rathaus lift!

🥨 Local Secrets

The English Garden is bigger than Central Park. Head to the Eisbachwelle to watch surfers in the middle of the city!

🛍️ Shopping & Essentials

Kaufland near the station is great for budget groceries. Most shops are closed on Sundays, so plan ahead!
