Getting Around Munich & Beyond

2026 Edition: Navigate the city like a local without overspending.

🚇 Public Transport Essentials

  • From the Airport:

    Take the S1 or S8 directly to Hauptbahnhof (~45 mins). An "Airport-City Day Ticket" (€17.50) is available if you don't have a D-Ticket.

  • Official Apps:

    Get the most modern and reliable way to navigate:

    Download MVGO App

    Avoid ticket machines; they often have long queues and confusing menus.

  • Validation:

    If you buy a paper ticket, you MUST stamp it in the blue machines before boarding. App tickets are pre-validated.

💡 Should You Buy the Deutschlandticket?

✅ Buy it if:
  • You are staying 7+ days.
  • You are staying 4+ days AND doing a day trip (Salzburg, Alps).
  • You have a Round-trip Airport Transfer (worth €35) + 3 days in the city.
  • You are traveling to multiple cities in Germany.
❌ DON'T buy it if:
  • Your stay spans two calendar months (e.g., June 30 – July 2). You would pay for both!
  • You are only staying in Munich for 1-3 days.
  • You prefer walking or only plan to use the train 1-2 times total.

🎟️ The "Cheat Code": mo.pla Deutschlandticket

The Deutschlandticket is a flat-rate monthly pass for all local and regional transport across all of Germany.

For travelers, the "Cheat Code" is using the mo.pla app. While most German apps require weeks of notice, mo.pla allows you to pause or cancel up to 24 hours before the month ends.

  1. Download:

    Download the official mo.pla app:

    App Store (iOS) Google Play (Android)

    Or check their official FAQ.

  2. Purchase:

    Buy the D-Ticket in the app (€63.00) via Credit Card or PayPal.

  3. Cancel or Pause Immediately:

    Once the ticket is in your app, you must act to avoid future charges.

    ⚠️ Critical Step

    Hit "Pause" or "Cancel" as soon as you see the ticket in your app. Your current month stays valid, but you won't be charged again. Most people forget this and get charged for a second month!

⛰️ Regional Travel & Warnings

⚠️ Warning: Regional tickets (D-Ticket/Bayern Ticket) are NOT valid on high-speed trains (ICE/IC/EC).

  • Red vs White:

    Stick to the Red regional trains (RE, RB) and the S-Bahn. Avoid the sleek White high-speed trains unless you have a separate long-distance ticket.

  • Key Destinations:

    Use your D-Ticket for day trips to Salzburg, Neuschwanstein, or Lake Starnberg.
