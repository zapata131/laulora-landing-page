Eat & Drink Like a Local

Welcome to Munich! Since you’re staying at the Hauptbahnhof, you’re in the perfect spot to explore the city's best eats—from traditional Bavarian feasts to the best late-night kebabs.

🥩 The Schnitzel Guide

While the Wiener Schnitzel (veal) is technically Austrian, Munich has perfected its own version. The Münchner Schnitzel is unique because the meat is often brushed with a mix of sweet mustard and horseradish before being breaded.

  • Wirtshaus Zum Straubinger:

    The upscale choice. A polished, traditional tavern perfect for a high-quality Schnitzel and other Bavarian delicacies in a classic setting.

  • Andy's Krablergarten:

    The best "bang for your buck." The portions are legendary.

    💡 Pro Tip: Custom Breading

    Order the regular schnitzel with mustard or horseradish baked into the breading for the most flavor.

  • Steinheil 16:

    The budget legend. Located in the university district (Maxvorstadt), it’s no-frills and serves a Schnitzel hanging off the plate.

🥨 Bavarian Breakfast (Weißwurstfrühstück)

A true local breakfast consists of Weißwurst (white veal sausage), a soft pretzel, and sweet mustard. It should be eaten before noon!

  • Augustiner Drei Mühlen:

    A cozy, authentic neighborhood spot away from the main tourist crowds.

  • Rischart at Marienplatz:

    Right in the heart of the city. Eating breakfast with a view of the Town Hall (Rathaus) is an essential Munich experience.

🍺 The Beer Garden Tradition

The Golden Rule: By law, you are allowed to bring your own food, but you must buy your drinks from the brewery.

  • Augustiner Keller:

    Very close to the hostel and Hauptbahnhof. It’s one of the oldest and most atmospheric gardens in the city.

  • Biergarten am Chinesischen Turm:

    A must-see icon in the English Garden.

    💡 Pro Tip: Kocherlball

    If you're here in July, look out for the Kocherlball, an annual tradition where thousands dance in traditional dress at 6:00 AM.

  • Hofbräukeller am Wiener Platz:

    Our favorite. It’s more local, less crowded, and a great excuse to explore the Haidhausen neighborhood.

  • Mini-Hofbräuhaus (English Garden):

    A hidden gem in the northern English Garden. Perfect to escape the crowds.

🌶️ Asian Flavors & Spice

Munich has incredible traditional food, but sometimes you need a break from the bread and potatoes. I love spice, and these are my go-to spots for authentic Asian flavors in the city.

  • TAKUMI München (Viktualienmarkt):

    Authentic Japanese ramen with rich, flavorful broths. Their spicy miso ramen is a personal favorite.

  • Chi Tu:

    Incredible Vietnamese street food. There are 4 branches in the city, but the one near Fraunhoferstraße is particularly easy to reach.

  • Max Beef Noodles:

    Traditional hand-pulled Chinese noodles. I highly suggest the Curry Noodles, though everything is fantastic.

    ⚠️ Cash Only: Like many gems in Munich, Max Beef Noodles does not accept cards.

  • 89Anju:

    A fun, k-pop infused Korean night with street food and snacks. Perfect for groups. (P.S. You can also ask me about any k-pop!)

  • Chaat Junction:

    A true hidden gem near Isartor. Authentic Indian street food that hits the spot when you're craving something spicy and flavorful.

🍟 Quick Bites & Late Night

  • Verdi Supermarkt:

    Just a couple blocks from the hostel. The Döner counter at the front is one of the best in the city.

  • Türkitch:

    Famous for modern, flavor-packed kebabs with fresh herbs and unique sauces.

  • Bergwolf:

    The ultimate after-party spot for currywurst and fries.

    🌶️ Spice Level: "Brutal"

    Try the traditional currywurst with spice level "Brutal" if you can handle serious heat; otherwise, stick to the regular option.

  • Burgermeister (Münchner Freiheit):

    A great budget burger option located in the lively Schwabing district.
