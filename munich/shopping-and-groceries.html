🛒 Shopping & Local Essentials

Staying at Wombat's City Hostel means you're right in the middle of everything. The catch is the hours: almost all shops close by 8:00 PM and are strictly closed on Sundays.

🛒 Shopping & Groceries

Where I buy food and basics, and what to do about Sundays, when almost everything is closed.

  • Lidl (Schwanthalerstraße)

    The closest big supermarket to the hostel. Go here if you're planning to cook in the guest kitchen.

  • Kaufland (Stachus)

    In the basement of the Galeria building at Karlsplatz. It's huge and has more international stuff than most shops. It's closed on Sundays, though.

  • Edeka (Hauptbahnhof)

    This is the one that saves you on Sundays. It's one of the few supermarkets in the city open 7 days a week. It gets crowded, but it has everything you need.

  • dm & Rossmann

    These are drugstores, not pharmacies. Go here for cheap toiletries, and they also have surprisingly good organic snacks (nuts, oat milk, vegan spreads).

🚨 Emergency Sunday Shopping

If it's Sunday and you're hungry, these are basically your only options near the hostel. Both get packed.

  • EDEKA (Hauptbahnhof)

    In the main station mezzanine. Open daily until 11:00 PM.

  • LIDL (ZOB/Hackerbrücke)

    At the Central Bus Station, about a 10-minute walk from Wombat's. The cheapest place to get groceries on a Sunday.

🛍️ The Main Shopping Streets

  • Kaufingerstraße & Neuhauser Straße

    The long pedestrian street between Marienplatz and Stachus. This is where the big chains are (Zara, H&M, TK Maxx), plus department stores like Oberpollinger.

  • Fünf Höfe (Five Courtyards)

    A really pretty set of courtyards on Theatinerstraße, with hanging gardens and a sphere sculpture by Olafur Eliasson. The shops are expensive, but I go just to walk through and see the art.

  • Maximilianstraße

    Munich's "Royal Avenue." Even if a luxury bag isn't in the budget, the Neo-Gothic buildings and fancy cars make it great for people-watching. At the end you get to the Maximilianeum, which is beautiful.

🏺 Speciality & Gourmet Shops

  • Dallmayr Delicatessen

    Skip the souvenirs and bring home some of their coffee instead. Everyone in Munich knows it. (If you have time, have a cup at their cafe too).

  • Manufactum

    They sell "things that still work": old-school, well-made stuff. The bakery inside, brot&butter, is so good.

  • Viktualienmarkt

    Go here for mustard, honey, and the famous spicy pickle from the Saure Ecke stall. Then take your snacks to the beer garden in the middle of the market.

💡 Good to Know

🍾 The Bottle Deposit (Pfand)

Don't throw away your plastic or glass bottles. Most have a €0.08 to €0.25 deposit. Take them to the "Pfand" machines at LIDL or EDEKA and you get the money back.

⚔️ Saturday is War

In Germany, almost everything is closed on Sundays, so do your shopping on Saturday (along with everyone else). If you forget, your only options for food are gas stations, the airport, or the supermarkets inside the big train stations (see the Sunday list above).
