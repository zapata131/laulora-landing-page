🏛️ Iconic Landmarks

  • Marienplatz & Neues Rathaus:

    The city's main square since 1158. Here you'll find the New Town Hall (Neues Rathaus) with its famous Glockenspiel.

    💡 Pro Tip: Alter Peter Views

    Climbing the 306 steps of St. Peter's Church for a few euros gives you the most iconic view of the Town Hall and the city skyline.

  • The English Garden (Englischer Garten):

    One of the world's largest urban parks. It's the locals' favorite place to relax, jog, or enjoy a beer.

    💡 Pro Tip: Chinese Tower

    Head to the Chinesischer Turm (Chinese Tower) beer garden for a classic Munich experience with a traditional brass band.

  • Eisbachwelle: City Surfing:

    At the southern edge of the English Garden, the Eisbach river forms a permanent standing wave. Skilled surfers ride the icy water day and night.

    💡 Pro Tip: Sunset Watch

    Stand on the bridge with a coffee or beer to watch the surfers. It's one of the coolest spots in the city.

  • Schloss Nymphenburg:

    The grand Baroque palace was the summer residence of the Bavarian monarchs. The expansive gardens are free to enter.

  • Frauenkirche:

    The famous twin-domed cathedral in the center of the city. It's one of the city's most recognizable landmarks.

  • Odeonsplatz:

    A large historical square inspired by Italian Renaissance architecture, framed by the Residenz and the Feldherrnhalle.
