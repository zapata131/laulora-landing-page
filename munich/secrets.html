Local Hidden Gems

Experience Munich off the beaten path with these unique spots and traditions.

✨ Unique Locations

  • Alte Utting:

    An old excursion boat permanently perched on a railway bridge. It's now a vibrant bar and cultural venue.

    💡 Pro Tip: Sunset Drinks

    It's an unbeatable spot for sunset. Try to get a seat on the bow of the ship!

  • Bahnwärter Thiel:

    An alternative cultural center built from shipping containers. Known for street art and club nights.

  • Gans Woanders:

    A magical "treehouse" cafe in the Giesing district, built with recycled wood.

🌲 Quiet Moments

  • Westpark Pagoda:

    A beautiful Thai Sala pagoda perched over a pond, much quieter than the English Garden.

  • Rubbing the Lion's Nose:

    At the Residenz on Odeonsplatz, rub the bronze lion's nose for good luck as you walk by!
