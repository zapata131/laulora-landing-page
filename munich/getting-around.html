Public Transport Essentials

  • From the Airport

    Take the S1 or S8 straight to Hauptbahnhof (about 45 minutes). If you don't have a D-Ticket, there's an "Airport-City Day Ticket" (€17.50).

  • Official Apps

    This is what I use. You can buy Day Tickets right in the app:

    Download MVGO App

    I'd skip the ticket machines. There's often a line and they're slow. The app is much easier.

  • Validation

    If you buy a paper ticket, you have to stamp it in the blue machines before you get on. App tickets are already validated.

Or just walk

Before you buy a ticket, check how far it is. The city center (Marienplatz, Odeonsplatz, Karlsplatz) is really compact, and for one stop, walking is usually faster and a lot nicer than taking the train. (I have some favorite walks on the Essentials page).

Should You Buy the Deutschlandticket?

✅ Buy it if:
  • You are staying 7+ days.
  • You are staying 4+ days AND doing a day trip (Salzburg, Alps).
  • You have a Round-trip Airport Transfer (two airport tickets already eat up a big part of the €63) + 3 days in the city.
  • You are traveling to multiple cities in Germany.
❌ DON'T buy it if:
  • Your stay spans two calendar months (e.g., June 30 to July 2). You'd end up paying for both.
  • You are only staying in Munich for 1-3 days.
  • You prefer walking or only plan to use the train 1-2 times total.

How I'd Buy the Deutschlandticket (Autobus Oberbayern)

The Deutschlandticket is a flat-rate monthly pass for all local and regional transport in all of Germany. If you're here for more than a few days, it can save you a lot of money.

The catch: it's technically a subscription, and the standard rule is that you must cancel before the 10th of the month or you get charged for the next one too. The only shop I've found that really solves this for visitors is Autobus Oberbayern: you can buy the ticket for the current month as a one-off, with the subscription cancelled automatically from the start.

  1. Open the Shop

    Go to the Autobus Oberbayern D-Ticket Shop (it works in the browser, no app download needed).

  2. Pick the Current Month

    Select the month you're traveling in, fill in your details and pay (€63.00).

  3. Keep "Single Ticket" Checked

    During checkout, make sure the option "Nur Einzelticket" (single ticket only) is selected. This cancels the subscription right away, so you only get the ticket for the month you chose.

    Don't skip this

    If you uncheck that box, the normal subscription rules apply (cancel before the 10th) and you could get charged for an extra month. Check it twice before you pay.

Regional Travel & Warnings

Heads up: regional tickets (D-Ticket/Bayern Ticket) are NOT valid on high-speed trains (ICE/IC/EC).

  • Red vs White

    Stick to the red regional trains (RE, RB) and the S-Bahn. Don't get on the white high-speed trains (ICE) unless you have a separate ticket for them.

  • Key Destinations

    The D-Ticket gets you to Salzburg, Neuschwanstein or Lake Starnberg for the day. (More on my Day Trips page).
