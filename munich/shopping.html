Shopping & Local Essentials

From the best supermarkets for a hostel-cooked meal to the famous walking streets. (I also mention some great market stalls in the Essentials page).

🛒 Essentials & Groceries

  • Budget Supermarkets:

    Look for ALDI, LIDL, or Netto for the best prices. Kaufland near the station is also great.

  • Drugstores (dm & Rossmann):

    Best for toiletries and healthy snacks. Their store brands are actually really good.

    🛑 The Sunday Closure

    Most shops are closed on Sundays! Plan ahead and shop on Saturday.

🛍️ Where to Shop

  • Kaufingerstraße:

    A long pedestrian zone with all major international brands, connecting Marienplatz and Stachus.

  • Viktualienmarkt:

    The best spot for local honey, traditional mustard, and artisanal food products. (Be sure to check out my specific food tips for the market in the Essentials page).

  • Maximilianstraße:

    Luxury shops and nice architecture. It's the most expensive street in the city.
